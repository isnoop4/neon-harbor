Neon Harbor is an original open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6, built for make.fun's "Make GTA 6" bounty. You are Rae Voss, a courier new to the sun-soaked harbour city of Neon Harbor, taking jobs from the fixer Marla Quinn. Explore the city on foot or by car, steal cars, dodge the harbour police and their wanted stars, fight with a pistol, and run four story missions — a delivery, a taxi theft, a police escape, and a warehouse shootout — with a HUD, a live minimap, and a save that carries your progress. Every name, character, vehicle, story and sound is original; nothing comes from Rockstar Games. The game runs entirely offline from static files in a desktop browser.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```

Controls: WASD / arrows to move and drive, E to steal or exit a car, mouse click to shoot, Shift to run, Q to save, C to continue a saved game, P to pause, M to mute.
